My approach is **pain points first, then framework.** Agile, Scrum, Kanban — whatever fits the team and the problem.

I don't force projects into boxes. Frameworks should drive products forward, not slow them down.

## What a healthy cycle looks like

1. PRDs that developers can actually follow
2. User stories with clear acceptance criteria
3. Backlogs that stay focused and prioritized
4. QA that's structured, not scattered

Every product needs its own system. I build the one that fits.
