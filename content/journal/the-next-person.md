Everything I build gets documented. Feature specs, user guides, technical notes.

The rule is simple: **the next person should understand the system without calling me.**

On the mena.tv platform that became a 120+ page Notion wiki — user guides, admin procedures, technical architecture, troubleshooting. The team operates the platform without needing me.

That was the goal.
