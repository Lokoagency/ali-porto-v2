For four years I translated between Arabic and English. Legal documents, serialized adventure stories for a language-learning platform, marketing copy.

Translation taught me something useful: **understanding what one side needs and making sure the other side gets it.** That's product work, really. Bridging gaps. Preserving meaning.

## Same craft, different medium

When I opened Bubble.io for the first time, it felt familiar:

- database structures instead of grammar
- color systems instead of tone
- user flows instead of sentence rhythm

The medium changed. The job didn't.
